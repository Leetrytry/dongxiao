# Dongxiao icon structure notes

These notes constrain the V4 icon redraw so the shape stays faithful to a traditional dongxiao/xiao instead of becoming a generic flute symbol.

## Structural constraints used for drawing

- Vertical end-blown bamboo tube.
- Nearly cylindrical body in front view; do not draw an obviously tapered or swollen tube unless intentionally drawing a root-bamboo Nanyin variant.
- Top end uses a natural node/end cap with an edge notch:
  - U-shaped or semi-circular notch for common xiao.
  - V-shaped notch for Nanyin dongxiao.
- Front view:
  - Draw exactly 5 front finger holes on the main axis.
  - Do not draw the rear thumbhole on the front face.
- Back / hidden details:
  - Traditional six-hole xiao/dongxiao has 5 front finger holes and 1 rear thumbhole.
- Lower end:
  - Draw lower vent/tuning/tassel holes as a pair or two pairs, positioned below the front finger holes.
  - If visible on the front icon, keep them visually separated from the 5 playing holes and smaller.
- Bamboo nodes:
  - Draw horizontal node rings/segments.
  - Node rings should not significantly change the outer diameter in icon view.
- Proportions:
  - Long, slender tube.
  - For a square launcher icon, crop/compress carefully while preserving slenderness.

## Reference facts

- EducationBao page: xiao made from a bamboo tube, vertically blown, top edge blow hole; front has five sound holes, back has one sound hole; lower back has two vent holes and two auxiliary holes that can also tie the tassel.
  Source: https://sjz.jiaoyubao.cn/edu/sjzyfzy/0_1161581.html
- Tianlong Art, Fujian Museum Nanyin dongxiao: V-shaped blowing notch, 6 finger holes arranged front 5 back 1, tail has 2 decorative holes for tassel, ten nodes and nine joints.
  Source: https://www.tianlong.art/Article/392.html
- Grinnell dongxiao: top end notch with sharp edge; five fingerholes, a thumbhole, and several vent holes.
  Source: https://omeka-s.grinnell.edu/s/MusicalInstruments/item/1251
- Yale Dong xiao: upper end closed by natural node with a notch; 5 + 1 finger holes plus holes for nylon tassels.
  Source: https://music.yale.edu/browse-collection/dong-xiao-31011973
- Oberlin xiao: slender bamboo cylinder, U-shaped top notch, five front finger holes, two lower tuning holes.
  Source: https://ohio5.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/knight/id/1731/
